How we work

A Florida company, cosmetic work only

Hayaku LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, document number L20000357012, filed in 2020, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations.

We do cosmetic work: cleaning, polishing and protective coatings. We are not a licensed repair facility, so mechanical work, collision repair and respraying go to licensed shops. We also will not coat over defects to make a car look good for a week — a coating locks in whatever is underneath it, and that is a conversation we have before starting rather than after.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Will this remove my scratches?

Some of them. Light swirls usually come out; anything you can catch a fingernail in is through the clear coat and will not. We will tell you which is which before starting.

Do you need my water and power?

No. We bring both. All we need is space to work and ideally some shade.

How long does a coating last?

It depends on maintenance and conditions far more than on the product. We will give you a realistic figure and tell you how to wash it so the figure holds.

Can you fix a dent or a scrape?

No, that is body work and goes to a licensed repair shop. We will tell you if that is what you actually need instead of selling you a detail.

How long will it take?

A wash and interior is most of a day. Correction and coating is often two. Anyone promising a full correction in an afternoon is not doing one.

Can you work at my office?

Yes, most of our work is done at homes and workplaces. We just need permission to be there and space around the vehicle.
