Terms of service

What we provide

Hayaku provides mobile auto detailing services provided by Hayaku LLC, a Florida limited liability company with document number L20000357012 on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations. The company performs cosmetic cleaning, polishing and protective coating only. It is not a licensed motor vehicle repair facility and does not carry out mechanical repair, collision work, refinishing or paint respraying, which are referred to appropriately licensed repair shops. Detailing is carried out with the vehicle owner's permission and the company does not work on a vehicle without the owner's authority. Paint correction removes a controlled amount of clear coat and is not always possible or advisable depending on paint thickness and prior repairs; deep scratches, etching, wear and pre-existing damage may be improved but not removed, and the company will say so before starting. Coating durability depends on maintenance and conditions. Wash water is managed in accordance with local rules on discharge to storm drains. Services are described on the Services page. No physical goods are sold or shipped through this site.
